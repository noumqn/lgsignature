Frequently Asked Questions

EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

What time is check-in and check-out at Hotel LG Signature Suites?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 12:00 PM. Early check-in or late check-out can often be arranged on request, subject to availability.

Is parking available at the hotel?

Yes, we offer free parking for guests at Hotel LG Signature Suites in Kalyan Nagar.

Do you offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel, including in every room and our co-working space.

Is breakfast included in the room rate?

Yes, breakfast is included with your stay, and we also have an on-site restaurant for other meals.

How far is the hotel from Manyata Tech Park and HRBR Layout?

We are just minutes away from Manyata Tech Park, HRBR Layout, Kammanahalli, Banaswadi, Nagawara, Hebbal, and Hennur Road, making us a convenient base for both business and leisure travelers.

Can I host a birthday party or small event at the hotel?

Yes, we have a dedicated party hall perfect for birthday parties and small celebrations, with personalised setups available.

Do you offer homestay-style suites for longer stays?

Yes, our homestay-style suites are designed for guests who need a comfortable, home-like space for extended visits.

What is the best way to get the lowest rate?

Booking directly through our website or by calling us at +91 9113929433 ensures you get our best available rate.
